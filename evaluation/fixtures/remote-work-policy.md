# Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely for up to 3 days per week with manager approval. Core collaboration
hours are 10:00 to 16:00 China Standard Time. Loss or damage of company equipment must be
reported to Information Technology within 24 hours.

员工经经理批准后每周最多远程办公 3 天。核心协作时间为中国标准时间 10:00 至 16:00。
公司设备遗失或损坏必须在 24 小时内报告信息技术部门。
