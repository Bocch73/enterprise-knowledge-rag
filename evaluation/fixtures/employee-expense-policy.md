# Employee Expense Policy

The daily meal allowance is capped at 120 yuan. Taxi fares after 22:00 may be reimbursed when
the employee records a business reason. Client entertainment expenses require written approval
before the event.

每日餐费补贴上限为 120 元。员工说明业务原因后，可以报销 22:00 之后的出租车费用。
客户招待费用必须在活动前取得书面批准。
