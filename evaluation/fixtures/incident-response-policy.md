# Incident Response Policy

A severity-one incident must be acknowledged within 15 minutes. During an active severity-one
incident, status updates are required every 30 minutes. A root-cause analysis must be completed
within 5 business days after resolution.

一级事故必须在 15 分钟内确认。一级事故处置期间必须每 30 分钟发布一次状态更新。
事故解决后必须在 5 个工作日内完成根因分析。
