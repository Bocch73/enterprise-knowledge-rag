# Employee Leave Policy

Full-time employees receive 15 days of paid annual leave each calendar year. Requests for
three or more consecutive leave days must be submitted at least 7 calendar days in advance.

全职员工每个日历年度享有 15 天带薪年假。连续请假三天或以上，必须至少提前
7 个日历日提交申请。

未使用的年假最多可结转 5 天，结转部分必须在下一年度 3 月 31 日前使用。
