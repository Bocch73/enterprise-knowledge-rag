# Procurement Policy

Purchases up to 5,000 yuan require team-lead approval. Purchases above 5,000 yuan and up to
50,000 yuan require director approval and at least three written quotations. Purchases above
50,000 yuan require review by the procurement committee.

金额不超过 5,000 元的采购需要团队负责人批准。超过 5,000 元且不超过 50,000 元的采购
需要总监批准并提供至少三份书面报价。超过 50,000 元的采购必须由采购委员会审核。
